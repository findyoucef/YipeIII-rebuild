What to listen for in the game
  • Battle music enters promptly; reward sounds have room above the score.
  • Leaving a fight restores the exploration track at its interrupted position.
  • Moving between regions of the same cave keeps the music continuous.
  • Cave rumbles, dripping water, fire and footsteps remain on the separate effects channel.

Use Options → Sound to adjust Music and Sound effects independently. These samples demonstrate the selected music; they are not a recording of a gameplay session.

Adaptations by icosium labs: static loudness matching, Ogg conversion, atmospheric loop crossfades, and brief edge fades where appropriate. Preview clips are excerpted, gain-adjusted and faded.
