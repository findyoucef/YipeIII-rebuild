**Draft**

The proposal is likely the smallest correct change:

- Add a per-call `blurMix = 1f` to `MiniatureEffect.Apply` and multiply only the final composite `amount` by it. Passing `0.3f` for maps 117–120 preserves the existing blur shape/radius—and therefore miniature depth—while restoring more original detail.
- Add a per-call final sampler choice to `HeatEffect.Apply`, using `PointClamp` only in Hell. Keep the initial enlargement and all non-Hell defaults unchanged.

**Correctness pitfalls**

- Do not multiply blur radius instead: smaller fractional radii sampled with `LinearClamp` can soften broadly without producing equivalent depth, and change both blur passes.
- `PointClamp` quantizes refraction. Because the source is already enlarged 4×, motion steps are quarter-native-pixel rather than whole-pixel, but subtle shimmer/stepping remains possible.
- Select the sampler before `DrawUserPrimitives`; changing SpriteBatch state does not affect the `BasicEffect` draw.
- Ensure every call derives both options from the same current-map check. Cached biome state could be stale during map transitions.
- Preserve exact defaults (`blurMix = 1f`, `LinearClamp`) and avoid changing render-target formats, blend state, blur generation, or non-Hell call arguments.

No clearly better equally small approach is warranted. Retaining linear heat sampling and reducing displacement would weaken motion but would not remove its interpolation softness. The proposed 4× point-sampled refraction is the better crispness/motion compromise.
