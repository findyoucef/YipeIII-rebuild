PRODUCTION
MonoGame game rendering; FFmpeg/libx264 video editing and encoding.
Capture uses staged isolated adventures and normal game actions. Ambient and
footstep recordings are mixed in post; combat hits and footfalls use captured event timing.
Location changes use short cross-dissolves. The Fire Cave approach and Flame Flier
fight are an uninterrupted capture of walking into an authored monster placement.
