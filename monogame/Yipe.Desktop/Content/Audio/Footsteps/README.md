# Recorded player footsteps

All eighteen footsteps are adapted from openly licensed **CC0 1.0** recordings.
They replace the initial synthesized sounds. Each material has three different
recorded contacts; there are no generated tones or artificial pitch variants.

| Files | Creator and source | Selected recordings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grass-0.wav`–`grass-2.wav` | [Nox_Sound — Footsteps_Walk.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Nox_Sound/sounds/490951/) | Dirty-ground contacts 2–4 |
| `stone-0.wav`–`stone-2.wav` | [Nox_Sound — Footsteps_Walk.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Nox_Sound/sounds/490951/) | Tile contacts 1–3 |
| `water-0.wav`–`water-2.wav` | [Nox_Sound — Footsteps_Walk.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Nox_Sound/sounds/490951/) | Barefoot-water contacts 1–3 |

| `snow-0.wav`–`snow-2.wav` | [Kenney — Impact Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | `footstep_snow_000.ogg`–`footstep_snow_002.ogg` |
| `sand-0.wav`–`sand-2.wav` | [Fantozzi — Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)](https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone), sliced/submitted by qubodup | `Fantozzi-SandL2.flac`, `Fantozzi-SandR2.flac`, `Fantozzi-SandL3.flac` |

Nox_Sound's publicly downloadable HQ MP3 preview was used. The original source
names, download URLs, segment offsets, processing gains and SHA-256 hashes are
in [sources.json](sources.json). The corresponding source pages identify all
three packs as CC0. Credits are retained voluntarily with the game content.

Adaptations: decoded to 22,050 Hz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV; DC removed; leading silence
trimmed; tails limited to 240 ms to fit the existing 250 ms walking cadence;
levels balanced with a peak ceiling of 0.75 and short boundary fades. Playback
uses the existing master volume × 0.28. Walking timing and gameplay are unchanged.

The [CC0 license text](CC0-1.0.txt) and Kenney's original [license notice](KENNEY_LICENSE.txt)
ship alongside the WAVs and this credit file in local builds and published output.
