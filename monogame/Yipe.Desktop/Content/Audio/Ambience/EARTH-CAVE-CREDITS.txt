58-second stereo 22050 Hz PCM16 loops. Existing cave air low-passed to 1100 Hz at half gain; dripping-water recording filtered 100–3800 Hz, gain 0.48 upper / 0.90 deep; quiet granular sample at three irregular offsets, no generated oscillators/noise. Final gain 2.0 with a 0.85 peak ceiling; two-second wrap crossfade. Mixed via build/earth-remaster/mix_audio.py; no peak normalization.
