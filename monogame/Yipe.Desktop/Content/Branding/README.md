# icosium labs studio ident

`icosium-labs.png` is the original transparent pixel-art company logo for the
port/remaster developer, icosium labs. Its stepped whitewashed houses, arched
passage, ochre terraces and sea-blue accent take their cue from the Casbah of
Algiers, as requested by the developer. Architectural reference:
[Archnet — Qasba, Algiers](https://www.archnet.org/sites/6089).

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The selected Casbah generation was
followed by a targeted lettering-color edit for readability on midnight navy.
No Python/image filtering or hand-replacement of the generated lettering was
used. Transparency is preserved. The game renders it on the native 320×180
canvas with point sampling and whole-number enlargement at supported sizes.
The ident does not receive world blur, vignette or scanlines. It fades in over
0.45 seconds and out over 0.55 seconds during its 3.2-second display.

The source PNG is shipped under `Content/Branding`, independent of the retro
terrain atlases. Original game credits stay separate from the port/remaster
credit in About. [Exact final prompts](PROMPTS.txt).
